Bundesliga Style Clash Cards

Coach-grade metrics, fan-readable narrative. Share this with friends before kickoff.

How to read: xG = expected goals, xGA = expected goals against, xG Delta = chance quality edge.
Metrics include: xG Delta, Shot Quality Conversion (Goals/xG), Defensive Suppression (xGA + shots conceded), Transition Threat, Set-Piece Threat, plus fan metrics (Goals/Match, Win Rate last 5, Shots on Target/Match).
