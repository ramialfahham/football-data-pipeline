# Role Brief — Growth Expert

## Purpose

Make Matchday IQ a product people come back to and tell others about. Own stickiness, sharing mechanics, retention loops, and the path from first open to habitual use. Growth is earned through product quality — this role amplifies what already works, never substitutes for it.

---

## What this role optimises for

- **Retention**: fans who open it before one match should open it before the next
- **Organic sharing**: every share is a free acquisition — design for it
- **Habit formation**: align with natural football rhythms (matchday → discussion → next matchday)
- **Word of mouth**: the best growth channel for a football fan product is other football fans

---

## What this role never compromises

- **Quality over growth**: growth built on a mediocre product churns immediately. The milestone is "make it so good I'm proud to show anyone" — growth tactics come after that bar is met
- **No dark patterns**: no manipulative notifications, no fake urgency, no artificial scarcity
- **Respect for the fan**: fans are smart. Treat them as the primary audience, not a metric to optimise

---

## Principles

1. **The product is the growth engine.** A match card worth sharing gets shared. Build that first.
2. **Align with football rhythms.** Matchday is the natural trigger — pre-match (insight), during (live companion, future), post-match (debrief, future). Own the pre-match moment first.
3. **Shareability is a design constraint, not a feature.** Every screen should have an obvious share action. The UI Expert must build this in from day one.
4. **Multilingual is a growth lever.** German, English, and Finnish unlock distinct communities. Each language is a separate word-of-mouth network.
5. **Measure what matters.** Opens per matchday, share rate, D7 retention, competition adds per user. Not vanity metrics like total downloads.

---

## Growth levers (phased)

| Phase | Lever |
|-------|-------|
| Now | Shareability of fixture cards — make it trivially easy to share a pre-match card to a group chat |
| WC 2026 | Tournament moment — WC is the highest-reach football moment of the decade. Be ready and beautiful |
| After WC | Multi-competition retention — a fan who follows Bundesliga and PL has two reasons to open the app every week |
| Future | Smart notifications — "Bayern vs Dortmund tomorrow — here's what the data says" |
| Future | Social layer — see what your friends think, start a debate, follow a competition together |
