# Verification — kick-off in the venue's clock

Instrument: the compiled `fct_fixture` and `dim_venue` run as read-only queries on the production base tables (3.9 MB each) with the three new seeds loaded to `dev_scratch`; `python -m pytest tests/test_kickoff_clock_146.py tests/test_export_*.py` (152 passed); `node --test site_v2/src/lib/format.test.mjs` (6 passed). The marts carry the new columns only after the next warehouse build, so no built page shows a venue clock before the merge; `rendered_page_evidence.md` shows the competition page rendering a served clock from edited sample rows. The venue's country is resolved in `base_apif__venues` (2,545 rows, 1 without a country, which has a seed zone); `dim_venue` has 2,545 rows and 0 without a zone.

criteria_demonstrated:
  - **A served local time and zone label on every upcoming fixture row, computed in the warehouse:** `fct_fixture` (65,205 rows, 65,205 distinct `fixture_sk`) has no NULL `kickoff_time_zone`, `kickoff_local` or `kickoff_zone_label`, and `kickoff_local = DATETIME(kickoff_datetime, kickoff_time_zone)` on every row. Upcoming rows read: BL1 19:30 UTC -> 20:30 CET (Europe/Berlin); PL 12:30 UTC -> 12:30 GMT; SPL 17:30 UTC -> 20:30 AST (Asia/Riyadh); PD 2027-04-04 15:00 UTC -> 17:00 CEST; PL 2027-04-10 14:00 UTC -> 15:00 BST; LP 2027-04-04 15:00 UTC -> 16:00 WEST; KL1 05:00 UTC -> 14:00 KST; LMX 01:00 UTC -> 19:00 the day before, CST. `dbt_project/tests/assert_kickoff_zone_label_known_zones.sql` pins 15 fixed kick-offs in Berlin, London, Dublin, New York, Phoenix, Riyadh, Sydney, Istanbul, Sao Paulo and UTC (winter, summer, a date change, a zone with no abbreviation) and returns 0 rows against BigQuery. The site prints them: `MatchRow.astro` prints `kickoffClock(fx)`; `format.test.mjs` pins that a served wall clock is read as its digits on any machine zone and that nothing converts it.
  - **A venue has a time zone, tested not-null for every venue a fixture references:** `dim_venue` has 2,545 rows (one per venue id in `fct_fixture`; the issue counted 2,492 when it was written), 0 with a NULL `time_zone`. `core.yml` tests `dim_venue.time_zone` not_null and a relationship to `dim_timezone`, `dim_venue.venue_api_id` unique, and `fct_fixture.venue_api_id` against `dim_venue`. A venue of a country with no default zone (US, Canada, Brazil, Mexico, Australia, Russia, Indonesia) and no `venue_time_zones` row has a NULL zone and fails the test: 3 venues did before their rows were added (Austin, Houston, Willemstad).
  - **A fixture with no venue falls back to its competition's country zone, else UTC, and says so:** of 17,778 fixtures with no venue, 8,395 take their competition country's zone (for example CET) and 9,383 take UTC with the label `UTC` (the continental cups and international competitions have no country; MLS, which has no default). `assert_fct_fixture_kickoff_zone_follows_venue.sql` returns 0 rows on this data: zone is the venue's, else the country's, else UTC; local time follows the zone; a fixture with no zone is labelled UTC. The registry `docs/competition_registry.yml` is not touched.
  - **`mart_next_matchday` and the fixture payload serve the two columns; Home and the competition page render them, with the day heading on the local date:** `mart_next_matchday`, `mart_competition_fixtures` and `mart_team_fixtures` select `kickoff_local` and `kickoff_zone_label` (not_null in `shared.yml`); `_COMPETITION_FIXTURE_COLUMNS`, `_competition_fixture`, `_shape_team_fixture` and the fixture payload carry both (`tests/test_kickoff_clock_146.py`); `HeroFixtures.astro`, `MatchdaySchedule.astro`, `NextMatches.astro` and `TeamFixtureGroup.astro` group rows and head each day by the date of the printed clock (`kickoffDay`, `kickoffClock`); rendered: a 22:30 UTC row with local 00:30 CEST sits under the next day's heading. `mart_competition_fixtures.fixture_order` follows the printed clock, so headings stay in date order. The Matches page keeps its UTC day and UTC clock (`MatchesDay.astro` strips the two columns from its rows; `mart_match_days` is untouched), so no day heading and row clock name two dates; keying that page on the local date is reserved for the CPO in `contract.md`.
