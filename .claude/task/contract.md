decisions_taken: >
  Six CPO rulings, 2026-08-11, all recorded on GitLab #57 (second note) before this branch existed:
    1. `display_group` is deleted from the seed — ⚠ **NOT DONE HERE**, see decisions_reserved.
    2. Naming rule: a type holding ONE specific competition is named for it; a type holding a
       CLASS is named generically. Hence `continental_club` -> `continental_cup` (it was the only
       type naming the ENTITY where every other names the FORMAT).
    3. TWO new club types, not one: `club_world_cup` (a tournament) and `intercontinental_super_cup`
       (a one-off between titleholders) — the same split the taxonomy already makes at domestic
       and continental level. No national-team intercontinental type: *"for national teams i don't
       know if we have intercontinental"*, and seeding a type on a guess is what #58 forbids.
    4. `single_country` added — it answers which registry field a row's sub-line reads. Nothing
       existing answers it: `entity_type` does not (a domestic league and the Champions League are
       both `club`) and `tier` exists only on leagues.
    5. `confederations.csv` stays a SEED rather than living in `strings.ts`: *"keep the seed, we
       may need it the warehouse later if not now"*.
    6. No blanks in the new columns, including the three friendly rows.

  9. THE 21 ENGLISH LABEL STRINGS ARE SHIPPED, AND MOST OF THEM ARE MINE. Stated plainly because
     the first draft of this contract reserved them for the CPO while the same diff shipped them
     as non-null, unique, test-enforced content — a self-contradiction a reviewer was right to
     fail. What is actually true:
       · CPO-attributable: the NAMING SYSTEM (a type holding one competition is named for it, a
         class takes a plural noun phrase — ruling 2), and 5 of the 14 category strings that
         follow directly from his own words: "Domestic leagues", "Continental club cups",
         "Club World Cup", "Intercontinental Cup", "World Cup".
       · BUILDER-AUTHORED: the other 9 category strings and all 7 confederation labels. Two are
         judgement calls rather than transcription — `FIFA -> World` (FIFA is a global body, not
         a region) and normalising CONCACAF to "North & Central America" where the registry's own
         `country` field writes "North/Central America".
     ⚠ NO PER-STRING APPROVAL WAS OBTAINED, and this contract does not claim otherwise. The CPO
     declined the review ceremony rather than the authority — asked to look at the list he
     answered that the point is the mechanism, not the words: the English IS the single source,
     one cell in one seed, translations keyed off it. §10 still makes user-visible wording his,
     so any of the 21 is his to change; nothing reads them yet (#62), so changing one costs one
     cell and no rebuild of anything that exists today.

  LABEL KEY CONVENTION, one builder decision: `label_i18n_key` = `compType` + PascalCase of the
  competition_type, mechanically, on every row — so `world_championship` takes
  `compTypeWorldChampionship` even though its label reads "World Cup". The key follows the TYPE so
  the two rename together; naming it after the label would leave the key stranded when ruling 2 is
  finally applied to that type.
