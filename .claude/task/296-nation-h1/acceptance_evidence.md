# Verification — Nation pages: an h1 of their own

criteria_demonstrated:
  - **Every nation page's h1 differs from the bare nation name in EN, DE and FI:** the h1 is the page's title string. On the built sample, Georgia's nation page reads "Georgia players" (`/en/players/georgia/`), "Spieler aus Georgia" (`/de/spieler/georgia/`) and "Georgia: pelaajat" (`/fi/pelaajat/georgia/`), read from `site_v2/dist` after `astro build`; the national team page's h1 is "Georgia" in all three. The country name stays English in DE and FI until #233.
  - **audit-seo reports no duplicate h1 on a nation page of the sample build:** with a national team page of Georgia in the sample, the old h1 gave 3 violations, all "h1 is not unique" on the Georgia pair (one per locale); with the new h1 the same build reported "2374 built page(s) checked. OK." and `check-built-pages.mjs` OK. That national team page is not in the committed sample: the design inventory measures it as a club page.
  - **A unit test fails when a nation page's h1 equals the bare nation name:** `node --test src/lib/playerIndex.test.mjs` passes 7 of 7 including the new test, which checks every nation page of the sample in EN, DE and FI.
  - **The live build:** the live build of 21 Nov (262,498 pages) had 285 "h1 is not unique" lines on nation pages. Computing the new h1 for each of the 285 nation names in its locale through `t(lang, "seoNationTitle")`: 285 of 285 differ from the team page's h1 and no two are equal within a locale. A full build from that export with the new h1 gives the audit-seo count recorded in the MR.
