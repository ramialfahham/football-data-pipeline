Nation page h1: `/en/players/georgia/` "Georgia players", `/de/spieler/georgia/` "Spieler aus Georgia", `/fi/pelaajat/georgia/` "Georgia: pelaajat".   - **audit-seo reports no duplicate h1 on a nation page:** before the change, the same sample build (2,373 pages) reported 3 violations, all "h1 is not unique" on the Georgia pair (one per locale). After the change `node scripts/audit-seo.mjs` reports "2374 built page(s) checked. OK." and `check-built-pages.mjs` reports OK.
  - **The live build:** the #156 session's live build (262,498 pages) is not on this machine, its dist lived in a container that is gone, so audit-seo was not rerun on it. Instead the new h1 was computed for every one of the 285 "h1 is not unique" lines of that build's audit-seo output (the nation name quoted in each line, in the line's locale, through `t(lang, "seoNationTitle")`): 285 of 285 differ from the team page's h1, and no two of the 285 new h1 are equal within a locale. A clash with an h1 of some other page of the live build is not excluded by this; the nightly's audit-seo run is the check.
  - **The sample holds the pair and a test fails without it:** `site_v2/src/data/teams/1104.json` (Georgia, entity_type national in `teams_index.json`) is committed beside the Georgia nation page's players; `node --test src/lib/playerIndex.test.mjs` passes 7 of 7, including the new test, which asserts at least one nation with both pages and that each locale's nation h1 differs from the team name.
