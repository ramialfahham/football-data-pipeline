# Rendered pages — #231 follow-up, a club name in a row

- **Instrument:** Playwright Chromium (the validate:ui browser) on two static builds of the committed sample, served locally: main 3dddb7af and this branch. Geometry is read in the page after its fonts load and the fit script has run.
- **The defect, reproduced:** the match page `/en/2-bundesliga/matches/2026-10-18-arminia-bielefeld-vs-vfl-osnabruck/` (and the DE and FI pages of the same match), Recent matches lists in `.rcol.fit-two`, with the opponent's name set to "Weston-super-Mare" and the competition to "FA Cup" in every result row. The sample has no such opponent, so the name is injected into the built page and the fit script re-run.
  - Before: the name `.cn` is 142.4px wide in a 166px column at 280px and the row overflows by 8px (scrollWidth 256, clientWidth 248); at 260px it overflows by 28px; at 320px it fits. The overflowing box is the club name; the competition and date wrap as whole words.
  - After: 0px overflow at 260, 280 and 320px, in EN, DE and FI. At 260px and 280px the name is 114px and 134px wide on two lines, "Weston-" over "super-Mare" in the narrow column; at 320px it is 142.4px, whole on one line.
- **Cause:** the build keeps a hyphen-joined word whole in a no-break span (`white-space: nowrap`). The name sits in `.club`, a flex item whose automatic minimum width is that whole word, so a row already in its last form (`fit-two`) has nothing left to give.
- **Sweep of the sample:** every built page in EN, DE and FI (786 per language), every tab, at 260, 280 and 320px, before and after (element-widths, summed over the three languages). 280 and 320px: every count is identical. 260px: every count is identical except one match page of each language, whose upcoming-match list has a hyphen-joined name wider than its line; the name now breaks at its hyphens (one "split at a hyphen" per language, one fewer "meta wraps" row and one more "name wraps" row), where it ran past its row before.
